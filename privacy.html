Privacy Policy

Pitlane — F1 Prediction Game · Last updated: 24 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pitlane (the “App”), a Formula 1 prediction game for iOS and Android, collects, uses, and protects your personal data. Pitlane is a free-to-play points game: you predict race outcomes and compete with friends. No real money is wagered and nothing of monetary value can be won.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Chouaib Mounaime, an individual developer based in France (the “Developer”, “we”).

Contact for privacy matters: privacy@pitlane.app (this mailbox is being set up — until it is active, you can reach us through the App’s support contact listed on its App Store or Google Play page).

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you provide

  • Account data — when you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your email address and the name associated with that account. With Apple you may choose to hide your email.
  • Profile data — username, display name, optional profile picture, favorite F1 team and favorite driver.
  • Game content — your race predictions, points, rankings, streaks, achievements, and the leagues you create or join.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Push notification token — a device token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send race reminders, results, and activity notifications you can turn off at any time in your device settings.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — via Firebase Crashlytics (crash traces, device model, OS version, App version), used solely to find and fix bugs.
  • Analytics events — only if you consent (see Section 4): anonymous usage events via Firebase Analytics, such as “prediction placed” or “league created”, used to understand how the App is used. Analytics collection stays off until you opt in.
  • Advertising data — the free tier shows ads served by Google AdMob. Depending on your consent choices (and, on iOS, App Tracking Transparency), ads are either personalized or non-personalized; AdMob may process device identifiers as described in Google’s policy.
  • Purchase data — if you subscribe to Pitlane Pro, payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We and our subscription processor (RevenueCat) receive only purchase metadata (product, purchase/expiry dates, subscription status, an account identifier) — never your payment details.

2.3 Data we do not collect

  • No precise location data.
  • No contacts, photos (other than the profile picture you choose), or files.
  • No payment card details (handled by Apple / Google).

4. Your consent choices

  • On first launch you are asked whether to allow analytics. Firebase Analytics stays disabled unless you accept.
  • Where required (e.g. EEA/UK), a Google-certified consent form (UMP) asks about ad personalization; on iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt follows.
  • You can change either choice at any time in Settings → Privacy (withdraw analytics consent, reopen the ads privacy options form).

6. How long we keep your data

  • Account and game data — kept while your account exists.
  • Account deletion — available directly in Settings → Delete account. Deletion removes your authentication record, profile, predictions, league memberships, uploaded images, and subscription records from our systems without undue delay.
  • Crash data — retained by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period (currently ~90 days) before automatic deletion.
  • Purchase records — Apple/Google retain transaction records under their own policies; our subscription metadata is deleted with your account.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, and receive a copy of your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). Most of these are available directly in the App (edit profile, privacy settings, delete account); for anything else, contact us (Section 1).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in France, the CNIL.

8. Children

Pitlane is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Where your country sets a higher age of digital consent (15 in France), that age applies. Pitlane involves no gambling: predictions are free and only score points.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over TLS and stored with access controls (including database row-level security) so that each account can only read the data it is entitled to. No system is perfectly secure, but we follow industry practices to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves (for example when new features ship). Material changes will be signposted in the App. The “Last updated” date above always reflects the current version.
